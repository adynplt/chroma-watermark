Light, captured and composed.

An ordinary page. Text, navigation and layout are untouched; only the image on the right carries the mark, embedded in the browser with the same shader the desktop demo uses.

Marked where it matters

The mark lives in the pixels of one element. A screenshot of the page carries it; the rest of the page carries nothing.

Invisible by design

A low-frequency blue-yellow shift, weighted by what the image can hide. Nothing changes in the text or the chrome.

Decoded with a crop

Point the decoder at the element's rectangle in the screenshot, or give it a loose box and let it search.
